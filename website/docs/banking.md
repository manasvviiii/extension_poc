# Investment Banking & Dealmaking Workflow

> [!NOTE] 
> **Status:** Implemented (Industry Workflow Specification)

In high-stakes deal sourcing, private equity buyouts, and investment banking M&A advisory, cold outreach yields response rates under 3%. Warm introductions via trusted mutual connections increase response rates to over 40%.

---

## 💼 Core Use Cases

### 1. Buy-Side Proprietary Deal Sourcing
Private equity deal teams use WarmGraph to identify senior partners or advisors who can introduce them directly to founder-owners before an auction process begins.

### 2. M&A Sell-Side Pitching
Investment banking pitch teams map relationship paths to C-level executives at strategic acquirers to secure mandate approvals.

### 3. Executive Search & Board Placement
Executive recruiters verify trusted backchannel reference paths prior to board-level candidate recommendations.

## 🎯 Warm Intro Drafting Guidelines

1. **Contextual Specificity**: Reference the specific bridge connector and shared deal context.
2. **Clear Value Proposition**: Keep the introduction request concise with a low-friction call to action.
3. **Double Opt-In Respect**: Always provide a clean forwardable blurb for the bridge connector.
