# Investment Banking Deal Workflow

**Category:** Investment Banking  
**Last Updated:** Sep 24, 2026  
**Reading Time:** 6 min read  

Investment bankers, private equity partners, and corporate development leaders use WarmGraph to accelerate buy-side and sell-side transaction sourcing.

---

## Transaction Use Cases

### 1. Buy-Side Deal Origination
- **Objective**: Reach C-suite executives or founder-owners of proprietary acquisition targets without cold emails.
- **Workflow**:
  1. Input target company name into WarmGraph target search.
  2. Discover existing warm paths through board members, portfolio executives, or mutual advisors.
  3. Request warm introduction from highest-ranked connector.

### 2. Sell-Side Purchaser Outreach
- **Objective**: Identify senior M&A or corporate development leaders at prospective strategic buyers.
- **Workflow**:
  1. Map target acquirers (e.g., Enterprise Tech acquirers).
  2. Filter by deal side (`sell_side`) and target role (`Head of M&A`, `VP Corporate Development`).
  3. Surface introductions via mutual private equity or advisory network.
